Principal's Message

"Education is the most powerful weapon which you can use to change the world." - Nelson Mandela

Dear Parents and Students,

Welcome to Vallalar Nursery and Primary School. Since 1991, our school has been a safe and happy place where young children begin their learning journey with care, love, and values.

We understand that the early years are very important in a child’s life. At our school, we focus on helping children learn through play, activities, stories, songs, and simple lessons. Our caring teachers make sure that every child feels safe, confident, and happy in the classroom.

We believe that each child is special. Along with basic learning, we help children develop good habits, manners, sharing, kindness, and confidence. Inspired by the values of Saint Vallalar (Ramalinga Swamigal), we teach children to be loving, respectful, and caring towards others.

Parents are an important part of a child’s growth. We work closely with you to support your child’s learning and development. Together, we aim to give your child a joyful and strong foundation for the future.

We look forward to welcoming your little ones and growing together as one family.

Child Education Methodology

குழந்தை கல்வித்தை முறை

Holistic Development

We believe in nurturing the complete child - intellectually, emotionally, socially, and physically. Our approach integrates academic learning with character building, creativity, and physical development.

Child-Centered Learning

Each child is unique with individual learning styles and pace. We personalize our teaching methods to accommodate different learning needs, ensuring no child is left behind in their educational journey.

Guided Discovery

We encourage exploration and discovery through guided activities. Children learn best when they actively participate in their learning process rather than being passive recipients of information.

Play-Based Learning

For our youngest learners, we use play as a powerful educational tool. Through structured play activities, children develop cognitive skills, social abilities, and emotional intelligence naturally.

Collaborative Environment

We foster teamwork and peer learning through group activities and projects. Children learn to communicate, share ideas, and work together towards common goals.

Continuous Assessment

We regularly monitor each child's progress through observation and gentle assessments, ensuring timely intervention and support when needed.

Age-Specific Educational Approaches

Nursery (3-5 years)

  • Sensory development through touch, sound, and visual activities
  • Language development through stories, songs, and rhymes
  • Basic motor skills through coordinated physical activities
  • Social skills through guided group interactions

Primary (6-10 years)

  • Structured academic curriculum with foundational skills
  • Critical thinking through problem-solving activities
  • Creativity through arts, music, and project work
  • Character building through value-based education
